# Training of Trainers: From Sequence Analysis to Teaching Practice 

As part of the EPIC project, the Robert Koch Institute is organizing a Training-of-Trainers (ToT) program for Bioinformaticians in Windhoek. The in-person training will take place from **25 November - 09 December** at the UNAM.

The aim of this Training-of-Trainers (ToT) program is to equip participants with the foundational knowledge and practical skills to become effective bioinformatics instructors by bridging the gap between technical and didactical competences. 

---

## Logistics

- **Dates:** 25 November - 09 December
- **Time:** The workshop runs from 9:15 to 16:30 each day, with a one-hour lunch break from 13:00-14:00. 
- **Catering:** will be provided. 

**Please bring your own laptop!**

---

## Workshop content

The curriculum covers three interlinked domains: 
1. learner-centered training design and facilitation;
2. genome reconstruction from sequence data; and
3. strategies for self-directed learning.

The in-person training will cover key components of a typical bioinformatics workflow for the analysis of raw long-read sequencing data from bacterial pathogens. The training will focus on practical, hands-on experience using Linux-based command line tools and commonly used bioinformatics software environments.

Main topics include:

- Linux command line basics
- Data and software management
- Conda environments
- Read quality control
- De novo genome assembly
- Assembly quality control
 
---

## 🎯 Learning objectives

At the end of this training and through active participation, trainees will be able to
1.	Apply combined competence in sequence data analysis and Accelerated Learning to design and deliver learner-centered training sessions.
2.	Run bioinformatics tools to quality control and reconstruct selected bacterial genomes from realistic toy sequencing data.
3.	Explain key bioinformatics concepts of pathogen genome reconstruction at a conceptual level.
4.	Apply six core learning principles that will enhance learner attention, engagement and retention.
5.	Select from a set of teaching activities to deliver collaborative, interactive and memorable learning sessions.
6.	Integrate supporting resources, such as Large Language Models (LLMs), to advance and sustain their continuous learning in bioinformatics.

## What should participants do before the workshop?

To make the most of the workshop, we kindly ask all participants to complete the following preparations in advance.

### 1. Propose a research project for the case study

The case study days are built around your own work. To make them relevant, we offer the opportunity to each trainee to propose a  sequence data analysis project, that is, a project that runs through the workflow covered in this training, from raw reads to a quality-controlled assembly (FASTA).

During the case study block (December 2-4), you will work in two groups on one of two projects selected from a pool of project ideas submitted before the in-person training. The purpose of this activity is to apply the tools, workflows, and analytical approaches learned during the first part of the training to a real dataset and research question. 

Please send a short case study proposal by November 2. Further information will be shared via e-mail.

Please note that when proposing your project, you are willing to share your dataset and work on it collaboratively with the group.

Instructors will review the proposals and confirm the selected dataset(s) by email.

### 2. Complete tutorials

Please complete the following tutorials available at [Terminal Tutor](https://www.terminaltutor.com/) and [Software Capentry](https://swcarpentry.github.io/shell-novice/index.html) :

- **Interacting with the Terminal**  

- **Navigating Directories**  

- **The Unix Shell**

We kindly ask you to complete the tutorials during the two weeks before the in-person component (11–24 November 2026), so that the content is still fresh when we meet.

Documentation can be found here: [https://code.visualstudio.com/docs](https://vscodium.com/#install)

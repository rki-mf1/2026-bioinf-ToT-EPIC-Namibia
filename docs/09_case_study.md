# 🧩 Case Study

During the case studies, participants will work in two groups on one of two projects selected from a pool of project ideas submitted before the in-person training.

The purpose of this activity is to apply the tools, workflows, and analytical approaches learned during the first part of the training to a real dataset and research question.

## 💡 Project proposals

Before the workshop, you are invited to propose a small research or surveillance project. Your project must include genome reconstruction, ideally of a bacterial genome from long-read (Nanopore) data.

Each proposal should include:
•	a brief description of the dataset
•	the organism or bacterial pathogen
•	the sequencing technology, such as Illumina or Oxford Nanopore
•	the main research question
•	the expected analysis or output

Projects should be clearly defined, technically feasible, and small enough to complete within two working days.

{: .important }
The case studies are intended for focused analyses, not large research projects. The research question should be specific and answerable within one day. The participants should propose only datasets on which they can work collaboratively - so please, avoid sensitive data which you cannot share with other participants. 

The trainers will review the proposals and select two projects. The selected topics will be announced before the in-person training.

---

## 👥 Group work

On the first and second case study day, each group will:

1. Review the research question and available data.
2. Define a realistic analysis plan.
3. Apply methods learned during the first part of the training.
4. Document the tools, commands, parameters, and results.
5. Interpret the findings and identify limitations.

Trainers will be available for consultations and troubleshooting, but each group will be responsible for its analytical decisions.

---

## 📊 Presentation of findings

On the second half of the third day dedicated for the case studies, each group will present its project and findings to the other participants and trainers.

The 30 min presentation should include:

* the project background and research question
* the dataset and analysis strategy
* the main results
* interpretation of the findings
* limitations and possible next steps

{: .discussion }
Did the available data and selected methods allow the group to answer the original research question? What additional data or analyses might be needed?

---

## 🎯 Learning outcomes

By the end of the case study block, participants should be able to:

1. Translate a research question into a practical analysis plan.
2. Apply methods learned during the training to a real dataset.
3. Work collaboratively on a bioinformatics project.
4. Document and interpret analytical results.
5. Communicate findings clearly to others.
